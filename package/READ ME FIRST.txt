JOB FINDER  -  tester build
==========================

Thanks for testing! jobslop searches lots of job boards at once, matches jobs to
your CV, tracks your applications, and (with its Chrome extension) fills in
application forms for you.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and an internet connection.
- If the window opens but stays blank: install "Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime"
  (free, from Microsoft's website). Windows 11 already has it.


HOW TO START
------------
1. Unzip this folder first (right-click the zip > Extract All). Don't run it from
   inside the zip.
2. Double-click jobslop.exe.
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because this is an unsigned test
   build. Click "More info" > "Run anyway".
   Your antivirus may also scan it for a few seconds the first time.
4. A short welcome tour walks you through the basics. You can see it again any
   time from Settings (the gear icon) > "Take the tour again".


THE CHROME EXTENSION (optional - for filling in application forms)
------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In jobslop, open Profile > "Set up for me".
   This opens Chrome's extensions page and the extension folder, and copies the
   folder path for you.
2. In Chrome, turn on "Developer mode" (top-right switch).
3. Click "Load unpacked", paste the path (Ctrl+V) and confirm.
4. That's it - it connects to jobslop by itself.
Press "Easy apply" on any job: the job opens in Chrome and a "Fill application"
button appears. It never submits anything - you always check and submit yourself.


YOUR PROFILE FROM A CV OR LINKEDIN
---------------------------------
- Profile > "Fill from my CV" reads a PDF or Word CV (UK, US, Dutch, Danish...).
- Profile > "Import from LinkedIn" takes your LinkedIn profile PDF (on LinkedIn:
  your profile > Resources/More > Save to PDF) or LinkedIn's data export .zip.
- If your profile already has details, you choose "Add to my profile" (keeps what
  you have, fills the gaps, adds new jobs) or "Replace my profile".


USE IT FROM YOUR PHONE (optional)
---------------------------------
1. In jobslop: Settings (gear icon) > "Phone access" > tick "Allow my phone to connect".
2. If Windows asks about the firewall, tick "Private networks" and click Allow.
3. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera (or type the address shown) and enter the
   6-digit PIN. Your phone stays signed in after that.
- Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi, and your computer on with jobslop open.
- From the phone you can search, save, track applications and edit your profile.
  "Apply" opens the job on your phone (form-filling needs the computer + Chrome).
- "New PIN" or "Sign out all phones" in Settings removes access at any time.


YOUR DATA
---------
- Everything stays on your own computer, in:  %APPDATA%\JobFinder
  (paste that into the File Explorer address bar to open it).
- Nothing is sent to the person who gave you this app.
- Job searches go directly from your computer to the job sites.


EXTRA JOB BOARDS (optional, free)
---------------------------------
Settings (gear icon) lets you add free keys for Reed (UK), Adzuna (19 countries),
USAJOBS (US government) and Apify (a backup for LinkedIn, Indeed and Glassdoor).
Everything else works without any keys.


TO REMOVE IT
------------
1. Delete jobslop.exe.
2. Delete the folder %APPDATA%\JobFinder.
3. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and remove "jobslop autofill" (or "jobslop Autofill") if you
   added it.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- LinkedIn is searched slowly on purpose; if LinkedIn asks to slow down, it pauses
  for 15 minutes.
- Job sites change sometimes - if one shows an error next to its name, the others
  still work.
- The map works offline ("Offline" switch); street maps need internet.
