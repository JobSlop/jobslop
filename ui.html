    Teach the app what a job can be called. Searching any of the names finds all of them, and “title only” means the word must be in the job title — so “communication” listed as a skill in an advert won't count as a communications job. Also used by “Match jobs to my CV”.

    Import from LinkedIn

      Import from LinkedIn

      Paste your profile link. jobslop opens it in Chrome, where the extension reads your profile — you need to be signed in to LinkedIn there.

      Or use a file
      1. Profile PDF (quickest): on LinkedIn open your profile → Resources (or More) → Save to PDF.
      2. Data export (most complete): LinkedIn → Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data → pick Profile, Positions, Education, Skills. LinkedIn emails you a .zip — use that.

      Welcome

      Welcome to jobslop

      One place to search dozens of job boards, match jobs to your CV, track every application, and fill in application forms for you.

      • 🔎 Search LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and many more at once
      • ✦ Match jobs to your CV and see how well each fits
      • 📋 Track applications and your progress
      • ⚡ Easy apply with the Chrome extension filling forms for you

      Takes about a minute, and you can skip any step. Everything stays on this computer — and anything you do can be undone with Ctrl Z.

      Start with your CV

      Where are you looking?

      Pick your country and town. You can change these any time with 📍 Mine or the 🗺 Map next to the search box.

      Searching

      • Type a job — use commas for several, e.g. chef, kitchen porter
      • Any job — no keyword, shows everything near you
      • ✦ Match jobs to my CV — searches your job titles and ranks results by fit
      • Filters — date posted, full/part-time, remote, minimum salary, nearest first
      • Job boards — tick or untick the chips under the search box; extra boards can be added with free keys in ⚙ Settings

      Apply & keep track

      • ☆ Save or ✓ Mark applied on any job — it goes on your Tracker board
      • Progress shows applications today, this month, your streak and interview rate
      • Changed your mind? ↶ Undo (top right, or Ctrl Z) puts back your last change
      • ⚡ Easy apply opens the job in Chrome where the extension fills the form with your profile and CV. It never submits — you always check and submit yourself.
